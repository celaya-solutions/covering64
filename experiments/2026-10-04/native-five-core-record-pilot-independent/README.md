# Independent five-core native pilot gate

**GO for the two frozen 300-second native calls.** Only root launches them.
The caps classify saved exact-64 records; they do not change the live walk,
its objective, its mutations or its stop rule. They refer to five named core
images, not to all relabelings. Qualification alone is already historical.

The two starts are f621 (H11/D25, new-core overlap 62 and ineligible for its
record cap) and 439d (historical H11/D27, already five-cap and weak qualified),
with seeds 2026105601 and 2026105602. The thresholds are 55 for each of four
old 60-block cores and 56 for the newly certified 62-block core. The weak
bucket requires exact cardinality 64, all five caps, H<=11, minimum pair count
at least five and D3=D4=0. Its rank is lexicographic (H,D2max); D2sum is a
reported diagnostic, not the rank. Raw64, complete and cap-qualified records
remain separate.

The kernel and core headers match the prior pilot byte for byte; the live
search loop is also byte identical. The reused pair evaluator matches its
previous frozen source. The new observer uses fresh counts and never updates
the live state or random generator. Added observation work can affect how
many steps fit inside the unchanged wall-clock budget.

A separately compiled zero-step copy of the real driver executes the actual
record lambda, with no call to the native advance routine. It checks 96 cap,
192 weak-qualification and 288 rank truth-table cases. Independent Python
counting matches seven real-family profiles. A pair-bad H9 fills raw/cap
records but cannot suppress weak H11/D27; H11/D25 then replaces that weak
record. Worse and equal ranks do not replace it. Cardinalities 63 and 65 do
not enter either exact-64 cap bucket. The complete 65-block control remains
saved. State, weights and random-generator values are unchanged. The
relabeled H11 fixture is a diagnostic control, not a new search discovery.

The separate Python review replays four saved zero/eight-step control outputs,
rejects four altered outputs, checks 11 fake runner cases and three watchdog
cases, and confirms the distinct initial-record policies for both starts.
No production native binary or optimizer was launched by either reviewer.

The pilot is limited to two sequential calls of 300 seconds each, a
315-second outer watchdog and five-second termination grace. It stops after
a checked complete family of at most 64 blocks, or on timeout/error/failed
validation. There is no retry or budget transfer. No search-completeness,
all-relabel novelty, covering existence or nonexistence claim follows.

[native-checks.json](native-checks.json) records the native delta and compiled
controls. [check_native.py](check_native.py) is the reproducible checker.
The Python review is in the sibling native-five-core-runner-independent folder.
