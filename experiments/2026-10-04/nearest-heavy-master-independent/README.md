# Preparation and outcome

The preparation gate rebuilt all 276 Boolean variables, 843 constraints, and
the soft objective directly from the heavy-block universe and checked cuts.
The complete protobuf matched. Two damaged controls were rejected. All input
hashes, cut provenance, source changes, stopping conditions, and budgets were
reviewed before the campaign ran.

The separate postcheck replayed all 95 incremental master models, candidate
responses, solver parameters, 697-row shifted completion models, numerical
primal vectors, and exact signed-dual cuts. All passed. The campaign added
95 exact fixed-pattern exclusions to the 238 input cuts, for 333 total.
It did not improve the best elastic objective or produce a fractional or
integer covering witness. No optimizer was called by either checker.

`audit.json` and `postcheck.json` bind the executed sources and result. Raw
models, numerical vectors, logs, and certificates stay in the ignored
nearest-heavy campaign folder. A master FEASIBLE status is a candidate,
not a proof of nearest-pattern optimality. The cuts apply to their stated
heavy-pattern family and do not prove a global covering-number bound.
